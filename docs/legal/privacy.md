## What we collect

### Information you give us
- **Enquiries and support:** your name, company, email address and what you write to us.
- **Purchases:** your name, company, email address, billing address, VAT number (if any), and what you bought. Our checkout provider takes your payment details. We never see or store your full card details.
- **Data you choose to send for support or services:** if you send us files, we handle them as clause 10 and Schedule 1 of our [Terms](terms.md) describe.

### Information we do not collect
- **Your transaction data:** FinLang is self-hosted. The engine runs on your own systems, and we do not see the financial data you process with it, unless you choose to send it to us.
- **Usage telemetry:** the FinLang engine contains no telemetry or tracking. It does not report back to us.

### Where we get it
- **From you,** when you contact us, buy from us or send us files.
- **From your organisation,** when a colleague names you as a contact for a licence or an Order.
- **From our checkout provider,** which passes us the details of your purchase.
- **At events and on professional networks,** when we meet or you give us your details.

### Do you have to give it?
No. But we need your name, organisation and email address to sell you a licence. Without them, we cannot complete the Order.

## Why we use it, and our legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis (UK GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Supplying the licence, rulepack, support or service you bought, including renewals and invoices | Contract |
| Answering your enquiries | Legitimate interests: responding to people who contact us |
| Following up with people we meet at events or who contact us about FinLang | Legitimate interests. You can opt out at any time |
| Keeping accounting and tax records | Legal obligation |
| Telling existing customers about FinLang updates relevant to what they bought | Legitimate interests. You can opt out at any time |

We do not sell personal information, and we do not make decisions about you by automated means.

FinLang Ltd operates from the United Kingdom. Some of our providers may hold data outside the UK. When they do, we rely on safeguards that UK data protection law accepts, such as UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

## How long we keep it

- **Enquiries:** 3 years from our last contact.
- **Payment and accounting records:** 7 years, for accounting and tax law.
- **Licence records:** while the licence lasts, plus 2 years. For perpetual rulepack licences, 6 years after purchase.
- **Data sent for support or services:** deleted or returned, at your choice, when the support or service ends, unless the law requires us to keep it.

---

## How we protect it

We use services that encrypt data in transit and at rest, and we limit access to the people who need it.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to:
- give you a copy of your personal information;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- restrict how we use it;
- object to our using it on the basis of legitimate interests, including for updates;
- give it to you, or to someone else, in a portable format.

Email info@finlang.io. We will respond within one month.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
